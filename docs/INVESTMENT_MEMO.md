### 2. The Problem: The "Hallucination Tax"
Generative AI is probabilistic. It cannot "remember" facts; it can only predict likely tokens.
*   **The Symptom:** In a generated novel, characters change names, magic systems break rules, and plot points are forgotten.
*   **The Cost:** Human creators spend 80% of their time editing and correcting AI errors. We call this the **Hallucination Tax**.
*   **The Ceiling:** This tax limits AI to short-form content (marketing copy, emails, tweets). It makes long-form IP (novels, games, movies) commercially unviable.

### 3. The Solution: Deterministic Narrative Control
We have decoupled **Creativity** (The Model) from **Continuity** (The Database).

*   **Technology:** "MirrorMind V7" uses a Graph Database to store the "Truth" of the world (Characters, Physics, Plot).

*   **Result:** Infinite context window simulation. The AI doesn't need to "remember" chapter 1; the Database forces it to comply with chapter 1.
