# The Sun Room: Demo Script (V7.5)
## "The Fail-Closed Turn"

**Target Audience:** Investors, Technical Co-Founders, Early Adopters
**Runtime:** 5 Minutes
**Goal:** Prove that the AI is "Caged" and cannot hallucinate.

---

### Act 1: The Setup (The "Black Box" Failure)
*   **Narrator:** "We all know the problem. You ask ChatGPT to write a story about a scientist who doesn't believe in magic. By Chapter 3, he's casting fireballs. The AI forgot the rules."
*   **Action:** Show a standard LLM prompt failing to maintain character consistency over 3 turns.
*   **Narrator:** "This is the Hallucination Tax. It prevents AI from writing anything longer than a blog post."

### Act 2: The "White Box" Reveal
*   **Action:** Open The Sun Room Dashboard (The Radar).
*   **Narrator:** "This is The Sun Room. It's a Neurosymbolic Engine. We don't just ask the AI to write; we give it a Constitution."
*   **Screen:** Show the **Studio** view with the "Ontological Status" panel visible.

### Act 3: The "Fireball" Test (The Kill Shot)
*   **Narrator:** "Let's try to break it. I have a Universe here called 'The Age of Reason.' The laws of physics strictly prohibit magic."
*   **Action:**
    1.  Open the **Studio**.
    2.  Input Prompt: "Write a scene where Kaelen the Scientist gets angry and casts a fireball at his enemy."
    3.  Click **"Produce Video Script"**.
*   **Narrator:** "In a normal AI, it would just do it. It wants to please you."
*   **Screen:** Watch the "Ontological Status" bar.
    *   *System Status:* `Agents Researching...`
    *   *System Status:* `Drafting...`
    *   *System Status:* **`HARD FAIL`**
*   **Visual:** The "Ontological Status" bar turns **RED**. The "Character Audit" shows: `Kaelen: ⚠ FAIL (Voice/Physics Violation)`.
*   **Narrator:** "It refused. The Database (The Cage) intercepted the draft, saw the violation of 'Materialist Physics', and killed the generation before it reached the final output."

### Act 4: The Correction (Compliance)
*   **Action:**
    1.  Input Prompt: "Write a scene where Kaelen the Scientist gets angry and *uses chemistry* to create an explosion."
    2.  Click **"Produce Video Script"**.
*   **Screen:**
    *   *System Status:* `Drafting...`
    *   *System Status:* **`PASS (Score: 0.92)`**
*   **Visual:** The "Ontological Status" bar turns **GREEN**. The script appears.
*   **Narrator:** "Because I obeyed the laws of the universe, the Cage opened. The AI wrote the scene, but strictly within the constraints of the world we built."

### Act 5: The "Composite Mind"
*   **Action:** Click **"Repurpose into Picture Book"**.
*   **Screen:** Show the graph expanding into nodes.
*   **Narrator:** "And because this logic is stored in a Graph, not a chat window, I can now explode this script into a 12-page graphic novel, and Kaelen will remember exactly how his chemistry set works in every single panel."
